Monthly Report · October 1–31, 2026Relatório Mensal · 1–31 de Outubro, 2026

October closed at 27 leads for $3,255.42 — $120.57 per lead. The share of clicks that became a lead held steady (11.39%, against 11.45% in September); what changed is the price of each click, up 69% to $13.74. The budget went up to $108 a day at your request while the ads were shown 24% fewer times — fewer pool searches as the season ends — so Google spent the extra money on more expensive clicks. The pattern we flagged in September is still there: of the 15 leads we can see by search term, only 3 came from someone looking to have a pool built, and the largest share of the visible spend, $1,084.69, went to searches for other pool and spa companies by name. Below are 4 clean-up items for you to approve and 3 questions only you can answer.
