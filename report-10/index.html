September closed at 38 leads for $2,704.88 — $71.19 per lead, the lowest cost per lead and the most leads of any month this year, on about $1,440 less spend than August. It is also the first month with the account running mostly on open (broad) keywords: broad took 76.5% of the spend and brought 33 of the 38 leads. One thing needs your eyes before we call it a clean win: of the 23 leads Google lets us see by search term, 15 came from people searching for another pool or spa business by name, or for hot tubs. Whether those calls were real pool buyers is something only you can tell us. Below there are changes for you to approve — tick the box on any line you want us to action.

Less money, more leads. Spend came down from $4,149.45 to $2,704.88 — the reduction you asked for, and then some — and leads went up from 30 to 38. Fewer people clicked (553 → 332) and each click cost more ($7.50 → $8.15), but more than twice the share of them became a lead: 11.45% against 5.42%. That is the usual shape when a budget is cut under Maximize Conversions: Google stops buying the marginal clicks and keeps the auctions it thinks are most likely to convert. “Lead Conversions” = tracked phone-call leads + lead-form submits, the primary actions we optimize for.
